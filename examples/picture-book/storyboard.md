Mara writes small things. A gate. A lamp. A bird that sits on the gate and will not move for anything.

Every night the bench fills with pages. The writing she knows. The making takes all night: a cover, eleven pictures, a voice, one long reel.

She never finishes before morning. So she stops, and she leaves a note.
:::
